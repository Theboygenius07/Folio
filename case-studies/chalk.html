1chalk for Mac

Ask about anything on your screen.

Hold ⌘⌥ and ask. When the answer needs a picture, a side board comes up beside your work, drawn from your own problem, and you can turn it, zoom it or keep it.

Say “show me on my screen” and chalk draws the working right there, over the app you’re in. Circle part of a board to ask about just that part. Ask about a PDF and it reads the whole document, not only the page in view.

2Pointing

It points at the real button.

A teacher on your screen lives or dies by where it points. If it says “click Chart” and lands on Text, you stop trusting the rest of the answer.

When chalk guessed the pixels from a picture of the screen, it landed on the right thing 59 times in 100. So I changed the question. chalk names the button, and code finds that button’s real box on the screen and puts the nib on it. That lands 97 times in 100.

3Maths

The answer first, then the picture.

Maths in chalk used to arrive as a wall of tiles and tables. Now it opens on the answer, as a sentence with the number big, then one picture, then only the sliders that matter.

The formula, the numbers put in and the totals wait under See the working, for when you want them.

4Learn by doing

Guess first, then it runs.

The fastest way to learn something is to be a little wrong about it first. So chalk asks you to draw the line you expect, then runs the real thing over it.

It checks your line the way a teacher would mark it: where it starts, which way it goes, where it crosses zero and where it ends.

5Remember it

Your turn, and a week later.

Every board ends with Your turn: one question, while the lesson is still in front of you.

A week later chalk asks again, in Today’s review. What you get right moves further apart: a day, then 3, 7, 21 and 60. What you miss comes back the next day, and anything you miss twice goes to the front.

6Your own course

Your lectures, your exam.

Make a course from your own lectures and past papers. chalk reads the pictures on your slides as well as the words, and answers with the pages it used, so you can check.

The Exam tab shows where the marks fall across your past papers, plans your revision to the day, and writes mock papers marked point by point, the way an examiner would.

7Videos

Ask for a video.

Ask for a video and chalk makes a short explainer: real pictures moving, chalk’s voice, and the words lit up as they’re said.

I also built cutroom, a video editor you talk to. It cut chalk’s launch film.

8The look

Drawn in code, in a crayon hand.

chalk’s feed and updates pages are drawn in code, never pictures: flat colour, white streaks where the wax skips, and grain over the lot. Every drawing is seeded, so it comes out the same every time.

Then the grain boils, stepping through 4 frames on a loop the way a hand-drawn film redraws its line. Every flower on this page is drawn that way.
